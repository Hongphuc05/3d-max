3DS MOOD - HUONG DAN CAI DAT PLUGIN CHO 3DS MAX 2022
====================================================

1. YEU CAU
   - Windows 10/11 64-bit
   - Autodesk 3ds Max 2022 ban tieng Anh (ENU), da mo len it nhat mot lan
   - Co ket noi Internet

2. CAI DAT
   a. Tat hoan toan 3ds Max.
   b. Giai nen file zip, chay PlatformSetup.exe.
   c. Neu Windows hien canh bao "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen):
      bam "More info" roi "Run anyway". (Phan mem chua ky so nen Windows canh bao, khong phai loi.)
   d. Doi cai dat bao hoan tat.

3. THEM NUT VAO TOOLBAR
   Mo 3ds Max: Customize > Customize User Interface > tab Toolbars.
   Category chon "Platform", keo nut "Mo Platform Design Library" ra thanh toolbar, bam nut de mo.

4. LAN CHAY DAU TIEN
   Plugin tu tai danh muc model va anh xem truoc tu may chu (can Internet, co thanh tien do).
   Lan sau chi tai phan moi them.

5. MUA THU VIEN
   Trong plugin bam "Mua thu vien" -> chuyen khoan theo ma QR hien ra -> bam "Toi da chuyen khoan"
   -> cho xac nhan (thuong vai phut). Khi duoc xac nhan, toan bo model duoc mo khoa.
   Mua mot lan, dung vinh vien, gom ca model them sau.

6. LUU Y
   - 1 license = 1 may. Doi may hoac cai lai Windows: lien he de chuyen license sang may moi.
